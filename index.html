Horse Bounce!

Drag and throw the horses. Click one for a dad joke or a horse fact. Find the rare golden horse!

Horses in the paddock: 0

Design Rationale

Horse Bounce is a silly, game inspired by website Cat Bounce. It was built with two plugins: Matter.js, a 2D physics engine, and Featherlight, a lightbox plugin. I wanted to show that plugins can be used to make something fun and full of personality, not just something useful. (ps i love horses)

  • Matter.js does all of the physics in this project. Each horse is a body with gravity, bounciness and friction, and the plugin handles collisions, so the horses bump into each other and bounce off the walls in a realistic way. Its mouse feature lets users grab a horse and throw it, which makes the page feel interactive right away. I only had to set a few numbers, like how bouncy the horses are, instead of writing my own physics code.
  • Featherlight shows a popup with a horse dad joke or a horse fact when a user clicks on a horse. I chose it because it is lightweight and easy to style, and it keeps the jokes hidden until the user finds them, which adds a little surprise every time. I also used it for this rationale, so the paddock can fill the whole screen without text getting in the way.
  • Together, they make a paddock full of flying horses telling dad jokes. The rare golden horse is the secret reason you’ll keep clicking “Make It Rain” way more times than you planned:D

Opportunities beyond the Proof-of-Concept:

For further development of this project, there are a few opportunities:

  1. Sound and music: each horse could neigh when it hits the ground, and a country song could play in the background, just like the music on Cat Bounce.
  2. Learning tool: the facts could be turned into a quiz for kids learning about animals, where users collect facts by catching horses.
  3. Custom animals: users could upload a picture of their own pet and make it bounce, or switch between different farm animals, each with their own jokes and facts.
